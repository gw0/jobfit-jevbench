**What You’ll Do**

* Perform site walkdowns to verify installed electrical systems against approved designs, single-line diagrams, specifications, submittals, and Lambda standards.
* Validate equipment ratings, protection settings, phasing, grounding and bonding, terminations, labeling, redundancy, and electrical failure domains.
* Identify installation deficiencies, design conflicts, and operational risks; document punch-list items and drive them through closure.
* Review construction test results and witness factory acceptance testing, site acceptance testing, functional testing, and integrated systems testing.
* Develop and review energization plans, MOPs, SOPs, EOPs, switching procedures, and electrical safety controls.
* Support initial energization and troubleshoot issues involving substations, transformers, switchgear, UPS systems, generators, busway, PDUs, RPPs, and rack-level distribution.
* Coordinate with design engineers, engineering partners, utilities, general contractors, electrical trades, equipment manufacturers, commissioning agents, operations, and HPC deployment teams.
* Confirm that electrical capacity, voltage, redundancy, monitoring, and protection are ready before IT equipment is energized.
* Validate as-built documentation, equipment settings, test reports, commissioning records, and turnover packages before operational handoff.
* Capture field lessons and incorporate them into Lambda’s design standards, commissioning requirements, and site-validation playbooks.
* Mentor other engineers and provide senior technical leadership during high-risk energization and bring-up activities.

**You**

* Have significant experience delivering, commissioning, or operating electrical systems in data centers, mission-critical facilities, utilities, or large industrial environments.
* Understand both medium- and low-voltage distribution, including transformers, switchgear, protective relays, generators, UPS systems, busway, grounding, and rack-level power.
* Can interpret single-line diagrams, equipment submittals, protection studies, relay settings, test reports, and construction documentation.
* Have hands-on experience with site inspections, electrical testing, energization, commissioning, or operational turnover.
* Understand electrical safety, lockout/tagout, energized work controls, and applicable NEC, NFPA 70E, IEEE, and NETA requirements.
* Can distinguish between cosmetic deficiencies and conditions that create material safety, reliability, or operational risk.
* Are comfortable making decisions in the field, escalating critical issues, and coordinating corrective work under schedule pressure.
* Communicate clearly with construction teams, engineers, vendors, operators, and senior stakeholders.
* Are willing to travel extensively and spend extended periods at active construction and commissioning sites.
* Hold a bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering or equivalent practical experience.

**Nice to Have**

* Experience commissioning hyperscale, colocation, HPC, or AI data centers.
* Experience supporting first energization and initial IT-load or GPU-cluster bring-up.
* Familiarity with Level 1–5 commissioning, integrated systems testing, and operational-readiness processes.
* Experience with protective-relay testing, breaker testing, insulation-resistance testing, power-quality analysis, or infrared inspections.
* Experience using tools such as ETAP, SKM, EasyPower, Procore, Bluebeam, or commissioning-management platforms.
* Experience investigating electrical failures and leading root-cause analysis.
* Professional Engineer licensure, commissioning certification, or NETA-related experience.
