**About Digital Asset & the Canton Network**
============================================

Digital Asset builds the technologies that are reshaping the foundations of financial markets. Our mission is to create the world’s most trusted, privacy-enabled, and interoperable digital infrastructure for global finance.

At the heart of this mission is the Canton Network - the blockchain purpose-built for institutional finance. Uniquely combining privacy, compliance, and scalability, Canton enables real-time, secure settlement across multiple asset classes. Governed by the Canton Foundation with participation from leading financial institutions, it is the proven link between the promise of blockchain and the power of global finance.

Founded in 2014 and headquartered in New York City, with offices in London, Zurich, Budapest, Hong Kong, and Sydney— we are on a mission to transform finance— and that transformation is now playing out rapidly on the Canton Network.

*If you’re interested in playing a crucial role at the intersection of traditional and crypto-capital markets and want to help bring the next trillion dollars of finance on-chain, join us.*

**About the role:**

The Staff Software Engineer is responsible for designing, planning, implementing, and testing solutions for Digital Asset’s products, working on large, complex, multi-component features and products, requiring team collaboration and a clear view of our customers' needs. The Staff Software Engineer understands DA products with both breadth and depth.

**Responsibilities:**

* Delivers high-quality and thoroughly-tested code, following our coding standards and organizational practices
* Independently and collaboratively delivers high-quality, fully tested advancements to DA products. The scope of work is 2 months or less.
* Breaks down large projects into smaller tasks that can be completed by other members of the team and communicates these tasks effectively both verbally and in writing
* Programs in multiple languages including but not limited to Scala, Haskell, Java, Go, and Daml
* Develops on MacOS, Linux, and/or Windows operating systems, with the primary deployment target of Linux
* Develops using Git and GitHub source code management tools
* Leverages product expertise to avoid potential problems, defining solutions and directing team members successfully
* Proficient in team processes and practices and recommends improvements to these processes
* Helps other team members attain the info they need to be successful and frequently mentors other team members

**What We're Looking For:**

* Expert in DA products. Has a broad and deep understanding of Daml
* MS or PhD in computer science or a related discipline or equivalent experience
* Strong understanding of scalable, distributed systems, and event-driven systems
* Strong  architectural and systems leadership and design thinking
* Able to accurately estimate projects
* In-depth knowledge in at least one of the functional programming languages, ideally Scala or Haskell.
* Highly competent in one or more domains of the Daml product and continually expanding their knowledge and expertise.  Able to troubleshoot and optimize both within and across domains.
* Committed to delivering high-quality solution
