Boston Dynamics seeks a highly technical **Software Engineer in Test**, to test the Atlas Humanoid Robot. In this critical, hands-on role, you will be responsible for ensuring the reliability, safety, and performance of Atlas’s software systems, focusing heavily on metrics based system testing for reliability and performance.

  

A key quality to be successful in this role is the exceptional ability to context-switch and rapidly acquire technical understanding across diverse domains: from web interfaces and network protocols to robot behavior and ML model validation. If you thrive on learning fast, solving cross-functional problems, and seeing your code directly impact a groundbreaking robot, this role is for you.

  

Boston Dynamics is a world leader in mobile robots, tackling some of the toughest robotics challenges. For years, our awe-inspiring [viral videos on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/BostonDynamics) have shown the world what remarkably capable robots can do. Now we are quickly becoming a recognized leader in automation solutions for industrial applications and warehouse logistics.  

  

**What You’ll Do:**

  

* **Test & Automation Design:** Design, develop, and maintain the comprehensive manual and automated overall regression test suite, focusing on integration stability. You will focus heavily on validating our teleoperation systems and action sequencing applications.
* **Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Testing:** Perform automated testing with physical hardware in the loop, executing actual sequencing setups, physical interventions, and real-time robotic test runs.
* **XR Applications Testing:** Drive the end-to-end quality of virtual environments by designing both automated and manual testing strategies for VR and Unity-based applications used in robot simulation and operator teleoperation.
* **Release Contribution & Reporting:** Contribute to generating detailed Test Execution Reports for major milestones and releases, clearly documenting coverage and communicating quality status to the manager and development teams.
* **Process Improvement:** Identify bottlenecks and gaps in the SQA process and testing coverage, driving continuous improvement in execution efficiency and reliability.

### 

**Required Experience:**

  

* 3–4 years of experience in Software QA Engineering, Test Automation, or Software Development, with a history of shipping and supporting product releases.
* Hands-on experience with **Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) manual testing**, including physical test setup manipulation and live sequencing execution on robotics, automotive, or embedded systems.
* Strong proficiency in test automation with a solid base in Python, C#, or a similar language. Direct experience automating **HTTP and WebSocket (WS) APIs** is required.
* Demonstrated experience testing **LLM / VLM systems**, including evaluating model responses via manual prompting, API layers, and setting up quality assessment metrics.
* Experience executing both automated and manual test strategies for **VR and Unity-based applications**, simulation software, or interactive 3D environments.
* Expertise in designing comprehensive test plans and executing functional, integration, and regression testing with strong manual rigor.
* Expertise in Defect Lifecycle Management using defect tracking systems (e.g., Jira) for precise defect reporting, verification, and analysis.
* Solid understanding of CI/CD practices and experience integrating and monitoring automated tests within continuous integration tools (e.g., Jenkins, GitLab CI).

  

**Nice to Have Experience:**

  

* Experience testing real-time control systems or low-latency teleoperation hardware/software.
* Familiarity with UI/UX and backend validation for complex workflow sequencing or orchestration applications.
* Operational familiarity with a Linux development environment (e.g., advanced bash/shell scripting).
* Experience with Root Cause Analysis on complex, cross-functional system failures.
