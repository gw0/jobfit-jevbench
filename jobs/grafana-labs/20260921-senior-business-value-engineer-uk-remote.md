**The Opportunity:**

Grafana Labs is seeking to grow our **Business Value Engineer** team and support defining, articulating, and quantifying business value across our products and services. As a member of the business value engineering team, you'll play a critical role in shaping the direction of this high-impact function.

**What You’ll Be Doing:**

* **Customer Insights**: Collaborate with customers to uncover new opportunities for growth and profitability through personalized storytelling.
* **Value Quantification**: Develop compelling ROI/TCO models that identify value areas driven by new products, emerging trends, and market challenges.
* **GTM Enablement**: Enable pre/post sales teams through training, content development and support; ensuring our sales organization is equipped to showcase Grafana's value with customers.
* **Value Storytelling**: Partner with cross-functional teams to create a unified narrative that showcases Grafana's value proposition, aligning internal stakeholders and external messaging.
* **Strategic Partnerships**: Collaborate closely with Account Executives, Solutions Engineers, Observability Architects to define and articulate the business value of our products and services.

**What Makes you a Great Fit:**

* **Innovative Mindset**: Possess a growth mindset, willing to learn new technologies and adapt quickly
* **Value Engineering Expertise**: 3-5+ years of experience in value management, consulting, or engineering; deep understanding of building quantitative and qualitative analyses.
* **Data Storytelling Skills**: Ability to analyze complex data sets, create compelling customer-facing stories, and communicate findings effectively from technical to executive level audiences.
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Experience partnering with GTM leaders (Sales, Customer Success, Marketing & Finance) to define and align on Grafana's value propositions.

**Bonus Points For:**

* 3+ years of Observability experience, preferably with Open Source tools like Grafana, Prometheus, or Loki
* Familiarity with the Open Source landscape and community

If you're ready for a high-impact role that combines data analysis, storytelling, technology enablement, and partnership-building with cross-functional teams – we'd love to hear from you!
