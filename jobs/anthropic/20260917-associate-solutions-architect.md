**About Anthropic**
-------------------

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

**About the Role**
------------------

We're looking for an Associate Solutions Architect to join our Applied AI team and help drive the adoption of Claude Code across our enterprise customers. As an Associate SA specializing in Claude Code, you'll be the technical bridge between our cutting-edge AI coding assistant and the engineering teams who can transform their productivity with it.

You'll partner closely with Account Executives to demonstrate Claude Code's capabilities, guide technical evaluations, and help engineering organizations successfully deploy and scale Claude Code across their development workflows. This is an opportunity to work with some of the world's most innovative companies that leverage AI to accelerate software development.

Responsibilities:
-----------------

**Drive Claude Code Adoption**

* Lead technical workshops on Claude Code for engineering teams and identify expansion opportunities for Claude Code with non-technical teams
* Guide customers through proof-of-concept implementations and technical evaluations
* Develop workflows and configurations tailored to customer development environments
* Focus on growing developer productivity for our existing customers using Claude Code

**Technical Customer Engagement**

* Conduct discovery calls to understand customer development workflows and pain points
* Run technical pilots that showcase Claude Code's impact on engineering velocity
* Provide hands-on support during customer evaluations and rollout phases
* Build technical champions within customer engineering organizations

**Product Expertise & Enablement**

* Maintain deep expertise in Claude Code capabilities, integrations, and best practices
* Create demo environments, scripts, and technical content for customer engagements
* Provide product feedback to engineering teams based on customer interactions
* Stay current on competitive landscape and differentiation strategies

**Cross-Functional Collaboration**

* Partner with primary Solution Architects and Product Engineers on technical aspects of sales cycles and collaborate on post-deployment expansion opportunities
* Work with Solution Architects and Product Engineers on complex technical requirements
* Contribute to internal knowledge sharing and best practices

You may be a good fit if you have:
----------------------------------

* 2+ years of software engineering or solutions architecture experience
* Daily active use of Claude Code with deep hands-on expertise
* 1+ years of customer-facing technical experience (sales, support, consulting, or similar)
* Strong understanding of software development workflows, toolchains, and developer productivity
* Experience with various programming languages, IDEs, and development environments
* Comfort presenting technical concepts and adapting your communication style for different technical audiences
* Experience gathering requirements and translating them into technical solutions
* Genuine enthusiasm for AI-powered development tools and their potential impact
* Strong problem-solving skills with ability to debug and troubleshoot technical issues
* Self-motivation with ability to manage multiple customer engagements simultaneously
* Growth mindset with eagerness to learn and adapt in a fast-paced environment

**How we're different**
-----------------------

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
