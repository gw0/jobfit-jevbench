**Job Description:**

We are seeking an experienced Channel Solutions Engineer to join our Partner team. The ideal candidate will have a strong technical sales background, cybersecurity expertise, and experience enabling partners to successfully position, technical sell, and deliver services. This role combines technical pre-sales, partner enablement, and solution development to drive partner-led growth.

 

**What will I be doing?**

As a Channel Solutions Engineer, you will work closely with partners, distributors, and internal field teams to build technical capability, build and accelerate opportunities, and increase partner self-sufficiency.

Responsibilities include:

* Aligning technical services with customer business needs to drive partner-led revenue.
* Delivering product demonstrations, workshops, and technical presentations.
* Building strong relationships with partner technical teams and acting as a trusted advisor.
* Enabling partners to develop repeatable solutions and service offerings around Darktrace technologies.
* Supporting partners on strategic opportunities through discovery, solution design, and technical guidance.
* Growing partner capability through coaching, mentoring, and technical enablement.
* Maintaining partner development plans that track capability, certifications, solution maturity, and business growth.
* Contributing to wider technical initiatives and sharing knowledge across the organisation.

**What experience do I need?**

We welcome applications from individuals with extensive experience in technical sales/solution engineering, ideally within a partner or channel ecosystem.

You will also likely have:

* Experience working with channel partners, VARs, MSPs, or distributors.
* Strong cybersecurity, networking, or cloud technology knowledge.
* Experience designing and presenting technical solutions to customers and partners.
* Strong competitor awareness and differentiation skills.
* Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and problem-solving abilities.
* A passion for developing partner capability and driving partner success.
* Be super proactive.

During the application process, you may be assessed on your understanding of Darktrace solutions, technical partner approach, and ability to enable partners to build scalable, customer-focused practices.
