**About the Role**
==================

We’re looking for a Lead Security Engineer to help build the security foundation. This is a hands-on role: you’ll set technical direction for security across our product and infrastructure, go deep in the code and the cloud yourself, and help shape the team that grows around you.

**What You’ll Do**
==================

* Own security architecture and the roadmap across our product and cloud environments, threat-modeling to focus effort where it matters most.
* Elevate our AppSec practice: secure the SDLC, establish code-review guardrails, manage dependencies and secrets, and implement pre-production security testing.
* Drive cloud security: IAM and least-privilege, account structure, network boundaries, and data-access controls.
* Lead incident response. This role requires participation in an on-call rotation and acting as the senior responder during incidents.
* Mentor and shape the security team and its operational processes.

**What You’ll Need**
====================

* 10+ years in security engineering, with deep expertise in at least two of AppSec, cloud/infrastructure security, and IR, and credibility in the third.
* A builder: you’ve created security capabilities and set direction, not only operated established programs.
* Strong cross-functional partnership skills — security should enable the business to build and operate safely.
* **Nice to have:** Consumer product at scale, AI/ML abuse or model-security exposure, experience as an early or founding security hire.
