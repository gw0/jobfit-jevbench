Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for an experienced **Senior Engineering Technician** to build, integrate, troubleshoot, and maintain complex robotic systems and engineering test assets. This is a hands-on role for someone who can work independently, own hardware within their area, and partner with engineers to drive problems to resolution.

**Responsibilities**

* Build, integrate, bring up, and maintain complex robotic systems, subsystems, and test equipment.
* **Independently troubleshoot electrical, mechanical, and system-level failures** using test equipment, logs, data, and engineering documentation.
* Own assigned hardware and test assets, including **configuration, upgrades, maintenance, uptime, and readiness**.
* Execute system and subsystem testing and clearly identify, document, and escalate failures.
* Work with engineering to reproduce failures, validate fixes, and drive issues to closure.
* Improve procedures, tooling, fixtures, and workflows to increase **reliability, quality, safety, and efficiency**.
* Maintain clear documentation of hardware configuration, test results, repairs, and known issues.
* Train and support other technicians on hardware, procedures, and troubleshooting.

**Required Qualifications**

* **5+ years of hands-on experience** building, testing, troubleshooting, or repairing complex electromechanical systems.
* Strong electrical, mechanical, and system-level troubleshooting skills.
* Experience with complex assembly, system bring-up, rework, and component replacement.
* Proficiency with diagnostic equipment such as multimeters, oscilloscopes, power supplies, logic analyzers, and DAQ systems.
* Working knowledge of Linux environments, shell commands, and Python for system bring-up, troubleshooting, test execution, and data collection.
* Ability to read electrical schematics, wiring diagrams, mechanical drawings, CAD, BOMs, and technical documentation.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience with robotics, automation, or complex electromechanical systems.
* Experience with system integration, reliability testing, or failure analysis.
* Familiarity with CATIA or similar CAD tools.
* Python, Java, and/or C++ experience.
* Knowledge of IPC-A-610, IPC-A-620, or J-STD-001.

The US hourly range for this full-time position is between $45/hr to $70/hr.
