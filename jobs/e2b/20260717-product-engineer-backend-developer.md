About the role
==============

Your job will be to own and scale the **backend that powers the E2B** [**Dashboard**](https://github.com/e2b-dev/dashboard) **and SDK** — the APIs, data, and services developers rely on to run, monitor, and debug their sandboxes. You're the backend counterpart to our dashboard engineers: they own the experience, you own the services and data behind it, and together you ship product end-to-end.

This is **product/application backend** - APIs, data layer, auth, billing, usage metrics, real-time log/metric pipelines. It is *not* our low-level infrastructure (Firecracker, orchestration, the sandbox runtime) - that lives with our Core and Platform teams, who you'll integrate with but not duplicate.

The split is roughly **80% Go backend, 20% TypeScript** — you live in Go, but you're comfortable dropping into the Next.js app to wire things up end-to-end and unblock the frontend rather than throwing work over a wall.

You'll build enterprise-grade services that scale to 100k+ weekly active users. Responsibilities include:

* Owning the Go services behind the dashboard and SDK — APIs, data models, and the contracts the frontend builds on
* Designing auth/authorization, billing, and usage-metering surfaces
* Building the real-time backends (WebSockets, SSE) that feed sandbox logs and metrics to the dashboard
* Optimizing data access and server-side performance — query design, caching, response times
* Partnering closely with dashboard engineers to ship features end-to-end, and with Core/Platform on the boundaries where product meets infrastructure
* Making architectural decisions around observability, testing, and security best practices

This role requires a high amount of ownership and the ability to take projects from 0 to 1. You'll have deep autonomy to make backend and product decisions. If you see a better way to build something, you build it.

What we're looking for
======================

* 3-5+ years shipping production backend services, with deep expertise in **Go**
* Strong API design — REST/RPC, contracts, versioning, and an eye for the interface the frontend actually needs
* Solid with **PostgreSQL**: schema design, query optimization, migrations, and reasoning about data at scale
* Backend fundamentals: authentication/authorization, server-side performance, and building real-time data flows (WebSockets, SSE)
* **Working TypeScript(Next.js is a plus) competence** — you won't live here, but you can move into the app-router frontend to wire up a feature end-to-end and not be lost
* Understand data fetching patterns on frontend side
* Excited to work in person from Prague on a devtool product
* Ownership mentality: you see projects through from conception to launch to iteration
* Comfortable talking to customers and explaining technical tradeoffs
* Not being afraid to take ownership of your part of the product
* Great communication, curiosity, and interest in the AI space

If you join E2B, you'll get a lot of freedom. We expect you to be proactive and take ownership. You'll be taking projects from 0 to 1 with the support of the rest of the team.
