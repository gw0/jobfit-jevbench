### **About Traversal**

[Traversal](https://www.traversal.com/) is the AI Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) for the enterprise—already trusted by some of the largest companies in the world to troubleshoot, remediate, and even prevent the most complex production incidents. Our mission is to free engineers from endless firefighting and enable them to focus on creative, high-impact work.

Our roots remain deeply embedded in AI research, and we’re channeling that scientific rigor and creativity into building the premier AI agent lab for the enterprise. Hence, what we’re proudest of is assembling the most talented yet nicest group of individuals, including researchers from MIT, Harvard, and Berkeley, to world-class engineers from industry: Citadel Securities, Cockroach Labs, Datadog, DE Shaw, ServiceNow, Glean, Perplexity, Pinecone, and more, to take on one of the hardest problems for AI to solve. Without the entire team, none of this would be possible.

### **The Role**

As a Go-To-Market Engineer, you will help build and scale our AI-native revenue engine. This role is at the intersection of growth, automation, and engineering. You will design and ship AI-powered workflows that generate qualified pipeline, remove friction across the customer journey, and help our marketing team operate with leverage through automation.

You’ll play a key role in developing a centralized marketing AI agent trained on our content, messaging, product knowledge, and performance data — enabling rapid creation of ads, social content, blogs, and creative assets with consistency and quality.

This role sits within Marketing and partners closely with Sales, RevOps, and Engineering. The right candidate will combine systems thinking with hands-on execution and a bias toward shipping.

### **Responsibilities**

* Build and ship AI-powered GTM workflows that generate qualified sales pipeline across outbound, inbound, and lifecycle channels
* Design and maintain a centralized marketing AI agent trained on company content, competitive intel, messaging, and performance data to accelerate content creation and campaign execution
* Own key GTM automation systems end-to-end, including prospecting workflows, enrichment, lead routing, scoring, and reporting
* Partner with Marketing, Sales, RevOps, and Engineering to identify friction in the customer journey and implement scalable solutions
* Design, test, and iterate on growth experiments, creating measurable feedback loops to improve conversion and pipeline efficiency
* Establish documentation, governance, and quality standards for AI and automation across the GTM organization

### **Requirements**

* 5+ years of experience in GTM systems, growth engineering, revenue operations, marketing automation, or a related technical role within B2B SaaS
* Strong hands-on experience building automation using APIs, webhooks, LLMs, and modern GTM tools (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot, Clay, Outreach, data enrichment platforms)
* Comfort working with technical systems including JSON payloads, APIs, and basic SQL for validation or analysis
* Proven ability to translate ambiguous GTM challenges into shipped, measurable improvements
* Experience building AI-powered workflows, agents, or automation systems that drive business impact
* Strong systems thinking and the ability to design scalable, repeatable infrastructure
* Excellent cross-functional collaboration skills, especially when partnering with internal engineering teams

### **Nice to Have**

* Experience building or deploying AI agents in production environments
* Familiarity with observability, developer tools, or technical B2B audiences
* Experience training LLM workflows on internal knowledge bases or structured datasets
* Experience supporting enterprise sales motions
* Prior experience as a Sales Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer or Account Executive
* Prior experience operating in a high-growth, early-stage startup environment
