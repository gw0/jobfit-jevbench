**Why We’re Hiring This Role:**
===============================

* More than 1 million developers have built 2.7M+ agents on Vapi. Every assistant depends on a growing product surface—from Composer, Simulations, Monitoring, and Knowledge Base to Versioning and Deployments.
* Behind those experiences are hard backend product problems: durable APIs and data models, real-time and asynchronous workflows, observability, evaluations, guardrails, and reliable operation of non-deterministic systems.
* We’re hiring a backend product engineer to own those systems end to end and help customers move voice agents from a compelling demo to dependable production—at Fortune 500 scale. The work is genuinely hard and unusually fun.

**What You’ll Do:**

* **30 Days:** Make real calls and build real assistants across LLM, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and telephony integrations. Develop a working mental model of how Vapi’s product surfaces map to APIs, services, data systems, and production workflows, then ship your first scoped backend product improvement.
* **60 Days:** Own a meaningful backend area in agent self-improvement, trust, or guardrails. Take a customer problem from product spec to shipped system—designing the API, services, and data model; defining evaluation and rollout criteria; and instrumenting the result. Use AI coding agents thoughtfully as a throughput and quality lever.
* **90 Days:** Operate as an independent owner for a customer-facing backend area. Run the full improvement loop—authoring, evaluation, deployment, observation, and iteration—while improving reliability and developer experience. Be a core voice in product decisions and have multiple meaningful improvements in production**.**

**Who You Are:**

**Must-haves**

* A track record operating at senior or staff scope: independently owning customer-facing backend product areas from an ambiguous problem through measurable production outcomes.
* Strong hands-on experience with TypeScript/Node.js, Go, or a comparable backend stack, plus the ability to design clear APIs, services, and data models.
* Experience building distributed or asynchronous systems with databases, queues, workflows, or event-driven patterns—and reasoning clearly about failure modes.
* Strong product judgment: you start with the user problem, turn incomplete requirements into a clear technical approach, and make tradeoffs based on customer impact.
* Production ownership: you care about testing, observability, reliability, incident response, safe rollouts, and what happens after launch.
* Regular, thoughtful use of AI-assisted development tools to improve speed and quality, paired with strong engineering judgment about the result.
* Self-direction, customer empathy, and clear communication. You stay hands-on, collaborate directly across functions, and remain accountable for outcomes.

**Nice-to-haves**

* Experience with Postgres and high-volume, event-driven systems using queues or durable workflows such as BullMQ, Kafka, or Temporal.
* Experience with real-time systems, voice or telephony, LLM-powered products, or AI agents.
* Experience operating cloud-native services on AWS and Kubernetes/EKS.
* Experience building developer platforms, public APIs, SDKs, or dashboards used by technical customers.
* Experience at a fast-growing startup, building a zero-to-one product, or founding a company.
* Open-source contributions, public technical writing, or other evidence of craft beyond your day job.
